4 Chargeable Weight & Weight Discrepancies

Freight is billed on the Chargeable Weight, defined as the higher of:

Chargeable Weight = Max(Actual Dead Weight KG, (Length CM × Width CM × Height CM) / 5000)

Parcels are audited on automated carrier conveyor scales. If the courier records a higher weight than originally declared by the merchant, the difference will be billed through the Weight Discrepancy module. Merchants have 48 hours to accept or dispute the discrepancy with photographic proof.
